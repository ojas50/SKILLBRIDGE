--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:00 - 0:45] HOOK / INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Walk to centre. Pause. Let the live site load on screen.)

Respected judges, mentors, and evaluators.

Every year, millions of young Indians walk out of polytechnics and vocational
institutes holding a degree -- and no job.

At that very same moment, another headline runs: "India faces a crippling
shortage of Cloud engineers, AI specialists, and cybersecurity analysts."

[pause]

Two truths. Same country. Same year.

And that contradiction is not a failure of talent. It is a failure of timing.
Our syllabus takes three to five years to change. The industry changes every
six months. By the time we finish teaching what WAS relevant, the market has
already moved on.

We are Team Syntax Squad, and we built SKILLBRIDGE to collapse that gap --
from years, to seconds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:45 - 1:45] CORE VISION -- THE 12-STAGE LOOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(gesture to screen)

SkillBridge is an AI-powered labour-market intelligence and curriculum-
alignment platform. At its core runs a continuous twelve-stage intelligence
loop: it ingests live employer hiring signals, extracts the skills industry
is ACTUALLY asking for, compares them against what our students are being
taught -- and closes the circle by feeding the result straight back into
curriculum design.

But a loop is only useful if someone can act on it. So the platform speaks
to three people directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1:45 - 3:00] KEY FEATURES -- THREE AUDIENCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(step forward)

For a STUDENT, SkillBridge answers one brutally practical question: "am I
ready?" Name the job you want, and the Career Readiness checker shows
exactly which skills you have, which you're missing, and which course closes
the distance -- with live demand figures like GenAI at plus forty-two percent
and Cloud & DevOps at plus thirty-four percent staring back at you.

For a GOVERNMENT DIRECTOR, it is the Executive Labour Cockpit: twelve hundred
courses, nearly four hundred detected skill gaps, district-wise placement
health -- and a What-If Simulator that models real decisions before a rupee
is spent. "What happens to placement if we move five hundred seats from a
declining course into Cloud DevOps?" The answer, calculated mathematically,
in seconds.

For an EMPLOYER, it is a validated partner network where curriculum is
checked against genuine hiring demand -- so the graduate who walks in already
knows the stack.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3:00 - 3:30] IMPACT / VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[pause]

The impact is not theoretical.

That three-to-five-year committee cycle for syllabus review? Under sixty
seconds.

Placement rates improve by fifteen to twenty-two percent when students train
on production-grade tools.

And public skilling money stops flowing into courses nobody is hiring from.

(look up)

Most importantly, this democratizes opportunity. A student in a Tier-3 town
gets the same market intelligence, the same roadmap, and the same chance as
someone in a metro -- because the data doesn't care where you studied.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3:30 - 4:15] CREDIBILITY + CONCLUSION / CALL TO ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We benchmarked against NEP 2020, NSDC skill-gap reports, NASSCOM FutureSkills,
and the World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs research. And what you see
behind me is not a mockup.

(turn to the live site)

It is live. Built on Next.js and TypeScript, deployed on Vercel -- and every
number on this screen is real.

Twelve stages. Three audiences. One loop that never stops learning what the
market needs, so our classrooms never stop teaching it.

SkillBridge doesn't just close the skills gap.

[pause]

It closes it IN TIME.

Thank you -- we'd be happy to take your questions.
